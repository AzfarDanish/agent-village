# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
v2.1. In short: be kind, be constructive, assume good intent, no harassment
of any kind.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior by opening a private channel to the maintainer
(see the repo profile for contact). Reports are taken seriously; maintainers
may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently remove contributors who violate
this code. Enforcement decisions aim to protect participants, not to punish,
and will be explained to the reported party.
